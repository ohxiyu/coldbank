# BIP39 English word list

`bip39-english.txt` is the canonical English list from Bitcoin BIP 39:

<https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039/english.txt>

BIP 39 declares the MIT License. The local copy is intentionally kept as a
plain, reviewable resource and must contain exactly 2,048 sorted words from
`abandon` through `zoo`.
